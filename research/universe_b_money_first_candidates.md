## 1. BID DESK — AI RFP/Tender Response Engine
- **WHO PAYS + CHANNEL:** GovCon SMBs, IT/SaaS firms, consultancies bidding government + enterprise RFPs. Reach: LinkedIn, APMP community, GovCon Slack/Discord, r/govcon, Upwork→direct, Deltek/GovWin-adjacent networks.
- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + CURRENT SPEND:** Proposal writers $75–250/hr; commercial RFP $2,000–8,000; complex/federal $10,000–50,000+; avg **33 hours per RFP** (Loopio benchmark). (FACT: hudson-bidwriters.com/proposal-writer-cost, spotz-sci.com)
- **WHAT AI DOES (70–90%):** bid/no-bid scoring, compliance/requirements matrix, first-draft sections from win themes + boilerplate library, past-performance tailoring, deadline/submission checklist. Human sells only final review + capture strategy.
- **WHAT THE HUMAN SELLS (outcome):** "Compliant, on-time submissions; more bids per month; 33 hrs → ~4 hrs."
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **1.6–1.7** (ESTIMATE) — $3,000/mo retainer ≈ ₹2.89L; ~5 hrs/mo (1 kickoff + final QA) → 1.7. One-off $2,000 bid → 1.6.
- **MEETING LOAD:** Low (kickoff call/client + occasional reviews; all async otherwise).
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** Low-Moderate. Client bid content is commercially sensitive but not bank data; no regulated advice; UPL not implicated. Do NOT consult for any bank/financial institution.
- **ROUTE:** Productised service → AI-operated → recurring bid-support retainer → software (compliance-matrix + draft engine).
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 8–12 clients × $2,500–3,000/mo, or white-label to capture consultants. Delivery by agents/templates; founder reviews only. No 10× hours.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** one paid "RFP teardown + full draft" at $1,000–2,000 to a GovCon found on Upwork/LinkedIn; convert to retainer.
- **EVIDENCE:** hudson-bidwriters.com/proposal-writer-cost/ ; spotz-sci.com/feeds/blog/request-proposal-consulting ; upwork.com/hire/proposal-writers/ ($60/hr, 168 jobs) ; fed-spend.com (GovWin $12k–42k/yr).
- **VERDICT:** **STRONG.** Best value-per-hour + strong recurring for frequent bidders.

## 2. GRANT ENGINE — Productised Grant Writing for Nonprofits
- **WHO PAYS + CHANNEL:** Small/mid nonprofits (US/EU) with 0–2 development staff. Reach: nonprofit Facebook groups, r/nonprofit, Candid/GuideStar lists, LinkedIn, Upwork→direct.
- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + SPEND:** Grant writers $50–150/hr; foundation proposal $1,500–5,000; federal $5,000–15,000+; retainer $1,500–5,000/mo; in-house writer $55k–75k salary. (FACT: grantsmarts.com/grant-writer-cost-guide/)
- **WHAT AI DOES (70–90%):** funder research/matching, first-draft narrative from intake form + past proposals, budget narrative, tailoring, LOI drafts, deadline tracking.
- **OUTCOME SOLD:** win-rate uplift + proposals submitted per month at 1/3 the price.
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **2.1** (ESTIMATE) — $2,000/mo ≈ ₹1.92L; ~4 hrs/mo → 2.1.
- **MEETING LOAD:** Low (one onboarding call per client).
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** Low. Not financial advice; percentage-of-award pricing is ethically barred (avoid it) — but flat/retainer is clean. No bank exposure.
- **ROUTE:** Productised service → AI-operated → recurring retainer → self-serve nonprofit portal.
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 10–15 orgs × $2,000/mo ≈ ₹19–29L; or 40 × $500 self-serve. Template + agent pipeline; add junior reviewer, not founder.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** free "grant-readiness scan" → 1–2 pilots at $1,000–1,500 flat per proposal from nonprofit groups.
- **EVIDENCE:** grantsmarts.com/grant-writer-cost-guide/ ; grantsights.com/blog/how-to-hire-a-grant-writer ; upwork.com/hire/grant-writers/cost/ ($35–60/hr; 152 live jobs).
- **VERDICT:** **STRONG.** Async, low liability, highly repeatable, clear recurring.

## 3. PIPELINE ENGINE — AI SDR / Prospect-List + Personalised Outreach

- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + SPEND:** Human SDR $7,500–10,000/mo loaded; AI SDR platforms $250–5,000/mo; cold email agencies $2,500–6,000/mo; LinkedIn agencies $1,500–6,000/mo; appointment setting $50–500/meeting, $2,500–15,000/mo; manual lead enrichment $15–50/profile. (FACT: aisdr.com/blog/what-is-an-ai-sdr, lead-spot.net, leadium.com, airtop.ai)
- **WHAT AI DOES (70–90%):** ICP list build + enrichment, personalisation, sequence send, reply triage, meeting booking.
- **OUTCOME SOLD:** qualified meetings / pipeline.
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **1.6** (ESTIMATE) — $2,000/mo ≈ ₹1.92L; ~3 hrs/mo → 1.6. (Rises if founder runs client's sales calls — cap scope to list+outreach only.)
- **MEETING LOAD:** Low for delivery; founder's own client-acquisition calls are the main load.
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** Low-Moderate. Deliverability + GDPR/CAN-SPAM compliance care needed; no bank conflict.
- **ROUTE:** Service → AI-operated → recurring → software (self-serve).
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 20–30 self-serve × $300–500/mo + 8 managed × $2,000/mo; platform-ise outreach.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** 2–3 pilot clients × $600–1,000/mo; hook = free "ICP + 100 enriched leads."
- **EVIDENCE:** aisdr.com ; cleanlist.ai (AI SDR $250–2,500+/mo) ; lead-spot.net/blog/b2b-appointment-setting/ ; airtop.ai (enrichment $15–50/profile).
- **VERDICT:** **STRONG** economics but commoditised — win on a vertical + real reply quality.

## 4. SIGNAL DESK — Competitive/Market Intelligence Subscription
- **WHO PAYS + CHANNEL:** SaaS product-marketing/founders, agencies, SMB strategists. Reach: LinkedIn, newsletters, Indie Hackers, agency white-label.
- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + SPEND:** CI analysts $30–100/hr (Upwork); market research firms $8,500 packages to $15k–50k/study; monitoring tools $10–99/mo. (FACT: upwork.com/hire/competitive-analysts, clutch.co market-research packages, preuve.ai)
- **WHAT AI DOES (80–95%):** continuous crawl of competitor pricing/site/ads/hiring/reviews, change diffing, weekly digest + battlecard. Pure agentic monitoring.
- **OUTCOME SOLD:** "always-on competitor radar + monthly battlecard."
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **2.6 at $1,000/mo; 4.2 at $250/mo** (ESTIMATE) — the lower the ACV, the worse the ratio → push tiered pricing.
- **MEETING LOAD:** Very low.
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes — designed async.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** Low. Public web data only.
- **ROUTE:** Service → AI-operated → recurring subscription → self-serve dashboard (software).
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 40–100 subscribers × $150–250/mo (≈ ₹10.4k MRR); shared agent infra = near-zero marginal hours. Most software-like of all candidates.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** free "competitor snapshot" lead magnet → 10 clients × $100/mo.
- **EVIDENCE:** upwork.com/hire/competitive-analysts ; preuve.ai/blog/how-much-does-market-research-cost ; visualping.io / priceva.com (monitoring tools $10–99/mo).
- **VERDICT:** **STRONG** — best scalability/SaaS path; risk is low ACV, so volume + tiers required.

- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** Low. Avoid owning placement/comp negotiation; no bank conflict.
- **ROUTE:** Service → AI-operated → recurring (monthly sourcing retainer) → software.
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 10 agencies × $1,000/mo white-label; or per-shortlist $500 × 20/mo.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** one shortlist at $500–800 for a recruiter on Upwork/LinkedIn.
- **EVIDENCE:** pin.com/blog/candidate-sourcing-companies ; upwork.com/hire/recruiting-assistants ; 1840andco.com/blog/outsource-recruiting.

## 7. ABSTRACTION DESK — Contract/Lease Abstraction & Data Extraction
- **WHO PAYS + CHANNEL:** Law firms, in-house legal, real-estate/lease admins, lenders. Reach: LinkedIn, legal-ops communities, white-label to LPOs.
- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + SPEND:** LPO abstraction $15–50/contract simple, $75–200+ complex; AI legal tools $200–500/user/mo; manual reads 90-page leases. (FACT: hyperstart.com/blog/contract-abstraction, superdupr legal, unframe.ai)
- **WHAT AI DOES (80–95%):** extract 40–60 fields, summarise, flag risk, populate a register.
- **OUTCOME SOLD:** clean reviewed contract/lease register + obligation calendar.
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **1.6** (ESTIMATE) — $2,000 project ≈ ₹1.92L; ~3 hrs → 1.6.
- **MEETING LOAD:** Low.
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** **Moderate-High — FLAG.** Confidential client contracts + legal privilege/UPL risk. Mitigate: extraction-only (no legal opinion), NDAs, or partner through lawyers. Not bank data.
- **ROUTE:** Service → AI-operated → recurring register maintenance → software.
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 15–25 firms; recurring "contract register" retainers; white-label to LPOs.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** one lease batch from a property manager (50 leases × $25).
- **EVIDENCE:** hyperstart.com/blog/contract-abstraction (LPO $15–50/$75–200) ; unframe.ai/blog/evaluating-ai-lease-abstraction-services ; superdupr.com (Kira/LawGeex $200–500/user/mo).
- **VERDICT:** **MODERATE.** Good value but confidential-data + UPL exposure; extraction-only or lawyer-partnered.

- **PAINFUL OUTCOME + SPEND:** SOC 2 readiness $8,000–25,000; first Type 2 audit $30,000–100,000; monitoring subscriptions from $49–400/mo. (FACT: scrut.io, soc2auditors.org, regulatorysignals.com/pricing, changeflow.com)
- **WHAT AI DOES (75–90%):** monitor regulator/framework feeds, classify impact, draft policy/control updates, evidence checklists.
- **OUTCOME SOLD:** "never miss a regulatory change; audit-ready."
- **FOUNDER HRS / ₹1L:** **5.2** (ESTIMATE) — $300/mo ≈ ₹0.29L; ~1.5 hrs → 5.2. Only viable at higher ACV or channel scale.
- **MEETING LOAD:** Low.
- **ASYNC-FIRST?** Yes.
- **CONFLICT/LIABILITY:** **Moderate — FLAG.** Compliance advice carries liability, and *financial-regulatory* topics overlap the founder's bank domain (perception + employer conflict). Stick to technical frameworks (SOC 2/ISO) and label output "monitoring, not legal advice."
- **ROUTE:** Service → AI-operated → recurring → software.
- **₹10 LAKH/MO MECHANISM:** 100+ SMBs × $99–299/mo, or white-label to fractional CISOs/GRC firms.
- **FIRST ₹1 LAKH PATH:** 5–8 SaaS startups × $150/mo; lead magnet = free "framework gap scan."
- **EVIDENCE:** scrut.io/hub/soc-2/cost-of-soc-2-audit ; soc2auditors.org/insights/soc-2-consultant-cost/ ; regulatorysignals.com/pricing.
- **VERDICT:** **MODERATE.** Recurring and productisable, but liability + bank-adjacency flag; confine to technical frameworks.

## DOMAINS SCANNED, NOT CARRIED AS FULL CANDIDATES
- **Healthcare admin (prior auth / medical billing):** money is real — prior auth $7–15/auth outsourced, automation $2–5; billing 4–10% of collections; $1,500–5,000/mo per practice; 13 hrs/wk of coordinator time (linear.health, carepatron.com, medcodexhealth.com). **REJECTED:** PHI/HIPAA = confidential client data (violates hard constraint), highest liability, US-entity/credential trust barrier = second-job risk.
- **Education/tutoring content:** low ACV, high support; not productisable at ₹10L/mo without headcount.
- **Logistics/freight back-office:** promising (doc processing) but heavy exception handling + carrier access = second-job risk.
- **Insurance claims processing:** manual $20–25/claim vs BPO $14/hr (acelerartech, helpware) — real, but regulated + claims decisions = liability.
- **Local services voice agents / AI receptionist:** platforms from $25–99/mo (cloudtalk, cryptopolitan) — highly productisable software but 24/7 support load = second-job risk unless fully self-serve.
- **Invoice/AP processing:** BPO $1.50–4.00/invoice vs in-house $15–26 (invoicedataextraction.com) — strong economics but confidential financial data; fold into a white-label channel only.
