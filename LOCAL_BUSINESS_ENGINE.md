# LOCAL BUSINESS REVENUE OPPORTUNITY ENGINE (top-priority outbound lane)
Objective: FIND a real business -> STUDY its public customer journey -> IDENTIFY a specific revenue/conversion/operating problem -> DESIGN a small custom solution -> CONTACT the owner -> EXPLAIN what we found -> OFFER to fix it.
Start: Hyderabad (easy to research, reachable owners, no enterprise procurement, observable problems, fast pilots). Expand to any geography with better economics.
DO NOT SELL "AI". Sell outcomes: more appointments, more qualified enquiries, faster lead response, fewer missed leads, more reviews, more repeat customers, better follow-up, more bookings, less staff time, better lead tracking, reactivation of old leads, better quote follow-up, better Google/Maps conversion. AI is the delivery mechanism, not the pitch.

## Revenue-leakage inspection (public sources only)
website · Google Business Profile · Google reviews (unanswered/negative) · enquiry flow · contact forms · WhatsApp journey · online booking availability · response options · Instagram/Facebook · CTAs · pricing/enquiry process · lead capture · appointment process · FAQs · review generation · follow-up · visible complaints · competitor journey · obvious repetitive admin.
Separate every finding: **OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN**. Never fabricate a problem.

## Categories to test independently
dental/specialty clinics · salons/spas/beauty · gyms/fitness/coaching · real estate brokers/builders · wedding venues/planners/photographers · home services · auto services · hotels/banquets · recruitment firms · (workers may add categories).
Prioritise where ONE additional customer is economically meaningful.

## Candidate offers (match to observed problem; do NOT pitch blindly)
missed-lead recovery · appointment/booking automation · dormant-lead reactivation (permissioned) · review engine · Google Business conversion improvement · enquiry qualification · quotation follow-up · lightweight sales pipeline/CRM (new→contacted→qualified→quote sent→follow-up→booked→lost) · FAQ/support handling with human escalation · custom workflow for a costly repetitive process.

## Outreach style
"I looked at your business and noticed something potentially useful." NOT agency boilerplate.
Structure: specific observation -> why it may matter economically -> very short description of the improvement -> low-friction CTA. Keep it short. Don't mention AI unless it helps.

## Show before asking
Small free artefact (3-point journey audit, redesigned enquiry flow, review-gap analysis, competitor comparison, follow-up sequence). Never large unpaid work. Purpose: prove the outreach is genuinely researched.

## Correct person
Owner / Founder / MD / Practice Manager / Centre Manager / Sales-Marketing Head — whoever can approve the pilot. Do not spray multiple employees at one company.

## Pricing discovery (do NOT assume Fiverr pricing)
Test: setup fee · monthly retainer · setup+monthly · per location · per qualified lead · per booking · performance component · managed service. Base on measurable customer value and delivery cost.

## Avoid commoditised services
websites, SEO, social posts, generic chatbots, generic lead lists, generic digital marketing — unless research shows an unusually strong opportunity. Always ask: "what measurable revenue/operating problem does THIS business appear to have?"

## Learn products from the market
CUSTOM OUTREACH -> discover repeating pain -> close pilots -> standardise delivery -> automate -> scale -> recurring revenue.

## Metrics per niche
targets researched · real problems identified · decision makers found · contacts verified · outreach sent · replies · positive replies · calls · pilots · revenue · average price · delivery hours · founder hours · repeatability.

## Priority order (immediate phase)
1 Customised local business outreach · 2 other high-value direct outbound experiments · 3 H2 marketplace test · 4 passive marketplace building · 5 speculative product building. Continue open-world discovery throughout.

## Relationship with H2
H2 stays as a Fiverr experiment (+ Upwork if approved) AND as an internal capability: if we can find and reach 1,000 businesses for a client, we should first use that capability to find the best opportunities for ourselves.
