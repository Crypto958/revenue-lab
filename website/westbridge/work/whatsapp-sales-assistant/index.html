WhatsApp Sales Assistant

Instant replies, FAQs, qualification, booking, follow-up and human handover — inside the chat the customer already uses.

The problem

Why this costs money.

For most Indian local businesses, WhatsApp is the main enquiry channel and also the one most likely to be read hours later, because it sits on the owner's personal phone alongside everything else.

What we built

The complete system.

A WhatsApp assistant that replies within seconds, answers the questions the business always gets asked, qualifies the enquiry with two or three questions, offers a booking, and hands over to a person the moment the conversation needs judgement.

Designed to improve

What this system is built to change.

These are the intended outcomes. We have not measured them at a paying client yet, so they are stated as designed objectives — not results, and with no invented percentages.

  • Response speed
  • Enquiry-to-booking rate
  • Out-of-hours coverage
  • Staff time on repeated questions
  • Follow-up consistency
Reliability & human handover

What happens when the AI should not be the one answering.

The assistant does not knowIt says so and asks a person to take over.
The customer wants a humanImmediate handover, no resistance.
The message is sensitiveIt is routed privately to staff, not answered automatically.
The customer opts outFollow-up stops immediately and permanently.
The booking system is unreachableThe lead is still captured for a callback.
