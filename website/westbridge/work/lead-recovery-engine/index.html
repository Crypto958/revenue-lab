Lead Recovery Engine

Automated follow-up and reactivation for leads that disappeared after the first enquiry or quote.

Find my revenue leaksAll systems
The problem

Why this costs money.

Most businesses lose more revenue to silence than to competition. The quote was sent, the customer got busy, and nobody ever circled back — so nothing looks broken while the money quietly leaves.

What we built

The complete system.

Every enquiry and quotation is tracked, and followed up on a schedule with short, relevant messages until the customer books, declines, or asks to stop. Follow-up stops immediately on request.

Capabilities in this build
  • Every quote tracked from sent to decided

Designed to improve

What this system is built to change.

These are the intended outcomes. We have not measured them at a paying client yet, so they are stated as designed objectives — not results, and with no invented percentages.

  • Quote-to-booking rate
  • Revenue recovered from existing leads
  • Follow-up consistency
  • Visibility of the open pipeline
Reliability & human handover

What happens when the AI should not be the one answering.

The customer asks to stopFollow-up ends immediately and is not re-enabled.
The customer has already bookedThe sequence stops the moment a booking is recorded.
The customer complainsThe contact is paused and escalated to a person.
