The quote that was never chased is the easiest money lost.

Automate the follow-up, keep the relationship.

Lead follow-up automation tracks every enquiry and quotation and follows up on a schedule until the customer books, declines, or asks to stop. Most local businesses lose more revenue to silence than to competition: the quote was sent, the customer got busy, and nobody ever circled back.

What we install

The enquiry that is answered first usually wins.

We do not sell software and we do not sell advertising. We install the five things that stop a business losing interest it has already paid for.

01 — Acknowledge

Every enquiry is answered within minutes.

Website forms, WhatsApp messages and missed calls all receive an immediate reply, day or night — including enquiries that arrive while the business is closed.

02 — Qualify

Two or three questions, asked automatically.

What the customer needs and when. Staff then spend their time on people ready to buy rather than sorting an inbox.

03 — Book

Appointments confirmed, then reminded.

Confirmation and reminder messages go out without anyone having to remember — which is what removes most no-shows.

04 — Follow up

Quiet quotes get chased.

If an enquiry goes silent after a quote, it is followed up on a schedule instead of quietly buying elsewhere.

05 — Ask

Reviews are requested at the right moment.

After a good visit, not in a bulk email. Your rating then reflects your work rather than who happened to remember to post.

What the research says

Response speed is the single biggest variable.

These are widely cited findings, not our own claims. They are the reason this service exists.

Responding within an hour

Harvard Business Review, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads (2011): firms that responded to a lead within an hour were roughly 7× more likely to qualify it than firms that waited longer.

Responding within minutes

The Lead Response Management study found contact and qualification rates fall sharply as response time rises from five minutes to thirty — the first few minutes carry most of the value.

What that means locally

For a clinic, venue or academy, the practical version is simple: the business that replies first usually wins the customer. Everything Westbridge does is aimed at that one variable.

Questions

Follow-up automation — common questions.

Is automated follow-up spammy?

It is the opposite. A short, scheduled, relevant follow-up is what a good salesperson already does. The rules are: few messages, each adding something, and an immediate stop on request.

Can it tell when a customer has already booked?

Yes. Once a booking is recorded the sequence stops.

How does this help with quotations specifically?

It removes the dependence on someone remembering. Every quote sent is tracked and revisited on a schedule.
