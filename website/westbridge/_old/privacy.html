Privacy

Privacy notice.

Short, because it should be readable.

This notice explains what happens to the details you submit through the enquiry form or send by email. It is written to be understood in one reading, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Details

What we collect, and why.

What we collect

Only what you send us

Your name, business name, phone number, email address, and optionally a website, city, business type, the problems you selected and your estimated monthly enquiry volume. We collect nothing automatically beyond ordinary web-server request logs.

Why

To answer your request

We use the details only to prepare and send the enquiry audit you asked for, and to respond if you reply. We do not sell, rent or share them with third parties, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

Who sees it

Us, and the form provider

Form submissions are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, so your details pass through that provider's servers on the way to us. Email is held in Google Workspace/Gmail.

How long

Until you ask us to delete it

We keep enquiries while the conversation is live and for up to twelve months afterwards for our own records, then delete them. Ask us at any time and we will remove your details sooner.

Your choices

Access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal

Email fca.abhi007@gmail.com to see what we hold, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent. We will action it within thirty days. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

None for tracking

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics is added later, this notice and a consent prompt will be updated first.
