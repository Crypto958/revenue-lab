Service · AI receptionist

An AI receptionist that answers in minutes.

For businesses where the phone is still the main channel.

An AI receptionist answers enquiries a business would otherwise miss — website forms, WhatsApp messages and missed phone calls — within minutes, day or night. For most Indian local businesses the person who currently answers is the owner or the front desk, which is exactly why enquiries are missed during consultations, classes and events.

The receptionist is the first layer only. It acknowledges, asks the two or three questions the business always asks, and routes anything that needs judgement to a person with the context already collected.

What the research says

Response speed is the single biggest variable.

These are widely cited findings, not our own claims. They are the reason this service exists.

Responding within an hour

Harvard Business Review, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads (2011): firms that responded to a lead within an hour were roughly 7× more likely to qualify it than firms that waited longer.

Responding within minutes

The Lead Response Management study found contact and qualification rates fall sharply as response time rises from five minutes to thirty — the first few minutes carry most of the value.

What that means locally

For a clinic, venue or academy, the practical version is simple: the business that replies first usually wins the customer. Everything Westbridge does is aimed at that one variable.

Questions

AI receptionist — common questions.

Is an AI receptionist the same as a chatbot?

No. A chatbot covers only the website. An AI receptionist covers WhatsApp and missed calls as well, which is where most Indian local business enquiries actually arrive.

Will callers know it is not a person?

The first reply is short and specific rather than scripted-sounding, and anything complex is handed to a human. Customers get a faster answer, not a worse one.

Does it work for a single-person business?

Especially. A one-person business is unavailable precisely when enquiries arrive.
