About

An audit habit applied to enquiries.

Why this exists.

Westbridge is an independent practice based in Hyderabad, India. It exists because the same failure turns up in business after business: an enquiry arrives, nobody owns it, nothing escalates, and no one notices until the customer is gone. That is a controls problem, and it is the kind of problem an auditor is trained to find.

The work is deliberately unglamorous. We read the public customer journey, name the specific defect, and then install the response layer that stops it happening again.

Questions

About Westbridge.

Who runs Westbridge?

Westbridge is run by Abhishek Singh, FCCA and MCSI, with around twelve years of experience in internal audit, risk and controls in financial services, most recently at Wells Fargo. The work is applying the same discipline — evidence, follow-up, no dropped items — to how a local business answers its enquiries.

Why does a controls background matter for lead response?

Because the failure is usually a process failure, not a technology one: an enquiry arrives, nobody owns it, nothing escalates, and no one notices until the customer is gone. Auditing is the practice of finding exactly that.

Which areas do you serve?

Hyderabad first, working directly with businesses. Delivery is remote, so other Indian cities can be served.
