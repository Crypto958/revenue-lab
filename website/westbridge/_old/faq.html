Enquiry response, lead generation and AI enablement.

What does Westbridge do?

Westbridge is an AI-powered enquiry response service for local businesses. It makes sure every website form, WhatsApp message and missed call gets a reply within minutes, then qualifies the enquiry, books the appointment, sends reminders and follows up on quotes that go quiet.

Who is Westbridge for?

Local service businesses in India where one customer is worth a significant amount: dental and skin clinics, IVF and specialty clinics, salons and spas, gyms, coaching centres, wedding venues and planners, photographers, real-estate brokers, hotels and banquet halls, home and auto services, and recruitment firms.

Where is Westbridge based?

Westbridge is based in Hyderabad, India, and works with businesses locally first. Delivery is remote, so other Indian cities can be served.

How much does it cost?

The enquiry audit is free. If you want the system installed, pricing starts at a ₹10,000 one-time setup plus a monthly fee from ₹5,000. Per-booking and per-qualified-enquiry models are also available.

Do I need to replace my website or my CRM?

No. Westbridge works with the channels you already have — your website forms, your WhatsApp number and your phone line. Nothing is replaced; a response layer is added on top.

How quickly can it be set up?

Typical setup takes days rather than weeks, because it connects to channels that already exist. The free audit comes first and asks nothing of your staff.

What is AI lead response?

AI lead response means software answers a new enquiry — from a form, a WhatsApp message or a missed call — within seconds and starts the qualification process, instead of the enquiry waiting for a member of staff. It matters because the business that replies first usually wins the customer.

How is this different from a chatbot on my website?

A website chatbot only covers the website. Most enquiries for local businesses arrive on WhatsApp and by phone, and the website form is often the smallest channel. Westbridge covers all three, and the goal is a booked appointment — not a conversation.

Will it sound like a robot to my customers?

The first reply is short, human and specific: it acknowledges the enquiry and asks what is needed. Anything complex is handed to a person. The system is designed to protect the relationship, not replace it.

Can it work with my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. Westbridge uses the business's existing WhatsApp number and website forms, so customers keep writing to the same place they always have.

What happens to enquiries that need a human?

They are flagged and handed over with the context already collected, so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

Does AI automation replace my staff?

No. It removes the repetitive part — acknowledging, asking the same three questions, sending reminders — so staff spend their time on customers who are ready to buy.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The enquiry audit costs nothing and carries no obligation. If what we find is not worth acting on, we say so.

How is the monthly fee decided?

It depends on the size of the business, how many channels are connected, and how much of the follow-up we handle. Most businesses fall between ₹5,000 and ₹40,000 per month, with a one-time setup from ₹10,000.

Is there a per-booking option?

Yes. Where a booking has a clear value, per-booking or per-qualified-enquiry terms can replace the retainer.

Is there a lock-in?

No. It continues only while it is producing bookings.

How do I stop losing customers to missed enquiries?

Attach a response layer to the channels you already have. Every enquiry — website form, WhatsApp message or missed call — should be acknowledged within minutes, qualified with two or three questions, offered an appointment, reminded before it, and followed up if it goes quiet. The single highest-return change is answering first.

How quickly should a business respond to a new enquiry?

Within minutes. In local services the first business to reply has a large advantage, because the customer is usually contacting several providers at once and books with whoever answers.

Can AI answer WhatsApp messages for a business in India?

Yes. WhatsApp is the primary enquiry channel for most Indian local businesses, and a response layer can acknowledge, qualify and book directly inside the same WhatsApp conversation the customer already uses.

What is the difference between AI enablement and buying software?

Software gives you a tool to operate. AI enablement changes the process so the tool runs without being operated — enquiries are answered, booked and followed up whether or not anyone is at a desk.

How much does it cost to automate enquiries for a small business?

In India, a focused enquiry-response setup typically runs from a ₹10,000 one-time setup plus ₹5,000 per month, rising with the number of locations and channels. The audit that precedes it is free.

Do small businesses actually benefit from AI?

They benefit from the outcome, not the label: replies sent in minutes rather than hours, appointments confirmed automatically, quotes followed up, and reviews requested after every visit. AI is simply what makes those steps affordable at small-business scale.
