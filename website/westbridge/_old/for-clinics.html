Clinics · Hyderabad

For clinics, an unanswered enquiry is a patient who books elsewhere.

Dental, skin, IVF, ortho and specialty practices.

Westbridge helps clinics in Hyderabad capture the appointment enquiries they already receive. For a clinic, one enquiry is worth ₹10,000 to ₹1,50,000 or more depending on the treatment. The leaks are consistent: a booking page with no working form, WhatsApp messages read after clinic hours, and no reminder before the appointment.

We connect the clinic's existing website forms, WhatsApp number and phone line to one response layer: instant acknowledgement, two or three qualifying questions, the appointment offered and confirmed, a reminder before the visit, and a review request afterwards.

What we find

The enquiries this business type loses most.

Observed on public customer journeys in Hyderabad — websites, listings, reviews and enquiry flows.

The booking page that does not book

A prominent “Make an appointment” button that opens a page with no form behind it — the most common single defect we find on clinic sites.

After-hours enquiries

Enquiries that arrive in the evening and are answered the next day, by which time the patient has often booked elsewhere.

No reminder before the visit

Without a confirmation and reminder, a proportion of booked appointments simply do not turn up.

Reviews not requested

Satisfied patients are rarely asked, so the rating under-represents the practice while unhappy ones post unprompted.

No view of the pipeline

Enquiries spread across phone, WhatsApp and website forms, with no single view of who is booked and who is still deciding.

Front-desk time consumed by repetition

Staff answer the same questions all day instead of handling patients in front of them.

What we install

The enquiry that is answered first usually wins.

We do not sell software and we do not sell advertising. We install the five things that stop a business losing interest it has already paid for.

01 — Acknowledge

Every enquiry is answered within minutes.

Website forms, WhatsApp messages and missed calls all receive an immediate reply, day or night — including enquiries that arrive while the business is closed.

02 — Qualify

Two or three questions, asked automatically.

What the customer needs and when. Staff then spend their time on people ready to buy rather than sorting an inbox.

03 — Book

Appointments confirmed, then reminded.

Confirmation and reminder messages go out without anyone having to remember — which is what removes most no-shows.

04 — Follow up

Quiet quotes get chased.

If an enquiry goes silent after a quote, it is followed up on a schedule instead of quietly buying elsewhere.

05 — Ask

Reviews are requested at the right moment.

After a good visit, not in a bulk email. Your rating then reflects your work rather than who happened to remember to post.

Questions

Hyderabad — common questions.

Can this work for a single-doctor clinic?

Yes. A single-location practice is often the best fit, because the doctor cannot answer messages during consultations — which is exactly when enquiries arrive.

Will patients know it is automated?

The first reply is short and specific. Anything clinical or complex is handed to the clinic's staff with the context already collected.

Does it handle appointment reminders?

Yes. Confirmation and reminder messages are sent automatically before the appointment.

What about patient confidentiality?

The system captures what the enquiry needs and routes it to the practice. Clinical detail is not handled by the automated layer; it goes to staff.

How much does it cost for a clinic?

The audit is free. Installation starts at a ₹10,000 one-time setup plus a monthly fee from ₹5,000, or per-booking terms for higher-volume practices.
