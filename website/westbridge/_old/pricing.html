Questions

Pricing questions.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The enquiry audit costs nothing and carries no obligation. If what we find is not worth acting on, we say so.

How is the monthly fee decided?

It depends on the size of the business, how many channels are connected, and how much of the follow-up we handle. Most businesses fall between ₹5,000 and ₹40,000 per month, with a one-time setup from ₹10,000.

Is there a per-booking option?

Yes. Where a booking has a clear value, per-booking or per-qualified-enquiry terms can replace the retainer.

Is there a lock-in?

No. It continues only while it is producing bookings.
