For a salon, a message that sits unread is a chair that stays empty.

Salons, spas and beauty clinics.

What we find

The enquiries this business type loses most.

Observed on public customer journeys in Hyderabad — websites, listings, reviews and enquiry flows.

Messages answered late

Enquiries arrive while staff are with clients; by the time they reply, the appointment has been booked elsewhere.

Manual booking confirmation

Appointments confirmed by hand, with no consistent reminder, which is the main cause of no-shows.

No reactivation of lapsed clients

Clients who have not returned in 60 or 90 days are never contacted, despite being the cheapest revenue available.

Reviews requested inconsistently

Happy clients are asked occasionally rather than every time, so ratings lag the real quality of the work.

Peak-hour overflow

Evenings and weekends — the busiest, most valuable hours — are exactly when replies are slowest.

No view of who is booked

Appointments scattered across DMs, WhatsApp and phone notes, with no single list.

What we install
