Coaching centres · Hyderabad

For a coaching centre, one missed enquiry is one lost student for a full year.

IIT-JEE, NEET, dance, music, sports and skill academies.

Westbridge helps coaching centres in Hyderabad capture admissions enquiries they are already receiving. One enrolled student is worth ₹80,000 to ₹1,50,000 per year, and typically far more across the full course. Yet enquiries usually arrive by phone only, at a time when staff are teaching, and nobody follows up the families who asked but did not enrol.

We install an enquiry form and a response layer across WhatsApp and phone: instant acknowledgement, capture of the course and the student's level, the next step, and structured follow-up on every family who enquired but has not joined.

What we find

The enquiries this business type loses most.

Observed on public customer journeys in Hyderabad — websites, listings, reviews and enquiry flows.

Enquiries only by phone

Teaching staff cannot answer calls during class, so a large share of admissions enquiries are simply missed.

No online admission form

Many centres have no way for a parent to enquire except by calling — which reduces the number who bother.

Scholarship and test registrants not followed up

Entrance-test registrations are a warm list, and are rarely worked after the test.

No view of the funnel

No record of who enquired, who visited, who tested and who joined, so non-joiners are never revisited.

Reviews not collected

Results and satisfaction are the strongest marketing a centre has, and are seldom turned into public reviews.

Batch underfill discovered late

Weak batches are identified after enrolment closes rather than while they could still be filled.

What we install

01 — Acknowledge
