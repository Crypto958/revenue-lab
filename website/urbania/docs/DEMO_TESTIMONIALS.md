The names, quotes, trip details, and ratings are not verified customer reviews. The six accompanying photographs were AI-generated for this demo and are stored as optimized WebP assets in `app/site/media/testimonials/`.

The section ends with the required disclosure: “* Demo testimonials and images shown for illustrative purposes.” Keep that disclosure whenever the synthetic dataset is shown. Do not add review schema, verification badges, or third-party review marks for this content.

When genuine customer feedback is approved for publication, replace the `DEMO_TESTIMONIALS` data in `build_sections.py` with consented, accurate review details and remove the demo-only rating and disclosure treatment as appropriate.
