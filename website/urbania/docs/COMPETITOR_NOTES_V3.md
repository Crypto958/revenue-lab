batta, state permit, GST) instead of publishing a single misleading "from ₹X/km".

## 3. New-scope observations (family group travel)

- **Family is treated as a bullet, never a page.** `hyderabaddeccantourism.com` says tempo travellers are
  "the preferred choice for family pilgrimage trips to Tirupati, Srisailam, and Shirdi". `srilaxmitravels.com`
  lists "Family Tours & Pilgrimages… Tirupati, Shirdi, Vaishno Devi". `ecosmobility.com` mentions "family
  vacations, pilgrimages". `indiancarrental.com` lists "family holidays, group tours". None of these is a
  *family-specific* page with fit, comfort, elderly/child, or luggage guidance.
- **The single best family/capacity content in the whole set is `urbania.rentals`** — its "Start with how
  many people are travelling" section explicitly answers "*where a bigger vehicle is the wrong choice*"
  and notes the 13-seater "keeps a full row free for luggage". It is the model to copy **for content**, and
  it again **does not cover Hyderabad**.
- **Family + outstation + pilgrimage collapse into one intent cluster** (Tirupati / Srisailam / Shirdi), which
  is exactly this operator's sweet spot for a single 17-seat van.

market almost entirely as **"pick a seater number"**, not as advice.

| `bookmytempotraveller.com` | 9/12/17/20/26/30 matrix with "best for" | Fine |
| `gozocabs.com` | 9/12/15 seater options | Fine |

**Mapping implied by the fetched pages (judgement, to reuse in copy):**
- **"vehicle for 10 people"** → a 12-seater (or a 10-seat "Maharaja"). A 10 means end-of-row wastage in a 9-seater; a 12-seater is the comfortable answer.
- **"vehicle for 15 people"** → a 17-seat Force Urbania (16+D) is the only single vehicle that seats 15 passengers **plus** luggage in one van; a 15/16-seater tempo is the budget alternative.

## 8. Is anyone in Hyderabad genuinely solving GROUP travel? — OPINION

**OPINION (not fact; my judgement from the pages I fetched):** In Hyderabad, "group travel" is served two
ways, and neither is *group transport as a product*.

1. **Vehicle rental / taxi companies** rent out *individual vehicles* (tempo traveller, Urbania, minibus,
   coach) by seat count and per-km/day rate — chikucab, gozocabs, hyderabaddeccantourism, taxiyatri,
   simplytrip, bookmytempotraveller, indiancarrental, srilaxmitravels, ecosmobility. They sell **"a vehicle
   for N people"**. Coordination of the *group* (multi-pickup, one point of contact, luggage logic,
   proactive fit advice) is not the product; it is at best a line item.
2. **Tour / travel agencies** sell *packaged trips* — the "Group travel in Hyderabad" directory results
   (e.g. ServiceBazzar listings) are all tour-package agencies, and community-trip players
   (e.g. Unusual Travel Company, from a LinkedIn/SERP snippet) sell *joining a group of strangers*, which is
   a different thing again.

**Conclusion:** in the fetched Hyderabad set, **nobody is genuinely solving group travel *logistics*.**
They solve either "buy a vehicle" or "buy a tour package". The genuine gap — one vehicle, one coordinator,
honest capacity guidance, multi-pickup, quote-first, family/pilgrimage/wedding/corporate handled with
logistics awareness — is **real but narrow**. It is a **positioning and content opportunity, not a moat**:
it is copyable the moment it works, and the underlying asset (one 17-seat van) is the same vehicle
everyone else can rent. Treat "group travel solved" as a *story to own and test*, not as defensibility.
