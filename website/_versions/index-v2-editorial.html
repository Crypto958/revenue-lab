Method

The enquiry that is answered first usually wins.

We do not sell software and we do not sell advertising. We install the five things that stop a business losing interest it has already paid for.

01 — Acknowledge

Every enquiry is answered within minutes.

Website forms, messages and missed calls all receive an immediate reply, day or night — including the ones that arrive while the business is shut.

02 — Qualify

Two or three questions, asked automatically.

What the customer needs and when. Staff then spend their time on people who are ready to buy, rather than sorting the inbox.

03 — Book

Appointments confirmed, then reminded.

Confirmation and reminder messages go out without anyone having to remember — which is what removes most no-shows.

04 — Follow up

Quiet quotes get chased.

If an enquiry goes silent after a quote, it is followed up on a schedule — instead of quietly buying from someone else.

05 — Ask

Reviews are requested at the right moment.

After a good visit, not in a bulk email. The rating then reflects the work rather than the luck of who remembered to post.
