## THE DECISIVE INSIGHT
Every business hypothesis shares the **same fatal dependency: cold distribution**. Cold reply rates are 1–5%, so even a good offer needs 50–100 well-targeted touches before the first yes — that is founder time, and it is exactly what the side-business constraint prohibits.
**Therefore the highest-probability move is not cold outreach at all — it is INBOUND: list the service where buyers already search** (Upwork / Fiverr / Contra / relevant marketplaces). Inbound removes the distribution bottleneck, needs no email addresses, and converts far better than cold.

## REVISED RECOMMENDATION
1. **Career (Tier 1)** — highest absolute probability of money; run applications properly. Watch the degree gap.
2. **H2 via marketplace listing (inbound), not cold email** — best side-business probability once distribution is solved.
3. Defer N1/N3 until inbound proves demand; they need creative production capability that is unproven here.
