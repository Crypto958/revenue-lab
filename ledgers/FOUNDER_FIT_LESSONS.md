## LAWS DISCOVERED (high confidence)
- **FIRM-GATE LAW (found independently 3×):** institutional buyers (banks, government/defence, enterprise) structurally exclude solo vendors — turnover floors, partner counts, empanelment, ITAR/CUI/FCL, security onboarding, procurement. Institutional revenue requires a FIRM as prime. [EVIDENCE]
- **MONEY↔ACCESS TRADE-OFF:** the segments with real budget are procurement-gated; the segments a solo can reach without procurement are cheap and crowded. [EVIDENCE]
- **COMMODITISATION LAW:** any public-data, no-procurement niche gets cheap clones ($5–199/mo) and a free DIY stack within the same period. Defensibility must come from vertical specialisation, accumulated history/data, distribution, or workflow lock-in — never from the technology. [EVIDENCE]
- **MEASUREMENT DEFECT (process law):** every founder-hours figure produced so far is MODELLED, not MEASURED. Mark `[MODELLED]`; accept no leverage claim until a pilot measures it. [INTERNAL]

## FILTERS
- WINNERS need: permissive licence (MIT/Apache/BSD) + PUBLIC data + AI end-to-end + buyer purchasable without procurement + async. [INFERENCE]
- LOSERS: confidential client data, bank/financial-institution adjacency, in-person T&M, independence requirements, deadline coupling. [EVIDENCE]
- HARD BLOCK: bank employment makes financial-institution clients a conflict (independence/competitor/moonlighting). Avoid all bank-adjacent revenue. [EVIDENCE]
