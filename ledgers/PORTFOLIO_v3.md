## DEMOTED / KILLED
- **D2 Model Risk — DEMOTED (near-kill).** W5: banks buy from FIRMS (juristic person, ₹50cr turnover, 10 partners, 15 certified staff); SBI RFP §28 **bans subcontracting**; work is in-person T&M = second job; serving-bank-employee conflict is disqualifying on independence/competitor/moonlighting grounds. One-off approved non-bank engagement only; legal sign-off first.
- **D3 Internal Audit/Controls — DEMOTED** (second-job; 25-40 hrs/₹1L).
- **D4 AI Governance — wedge only.**
- **KILLED:** O6 audit-firm evidence service; Ledger Ops (confidential financial data, bank-adjacent); healthcare prior-auth (PHI/HIPAA); insurance claims (liability); content/YouTube/POD.

## Design rule learned
WINNERS = permissive licence (MIT/Apache/BSD) + PUBLIC (non-confidential) data + AI runs the process end-to-end → 10-25 founder-hrs per ₹1L instead of 50-70. Confidential-data or bank-adjacent = conflict + liability → avoid.
