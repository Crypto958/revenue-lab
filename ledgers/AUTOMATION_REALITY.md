# AUTOMATION REALITY (what AI can actually do reliably)
Separate 'AI can draft' from 'AI can be trusted to submit'.

## Reliable today (LOW QA burden)
- Structured extraction from documents with a fixed schema (fields, dates, amounts).
- First-draft narrative from boilerplate + source material.
- Crawling public pages and diffing changes (price/site/hiring/reviews).
- Classification, tagging, summarisation, list-building/enrichment.
- Template-driven document assembly.

## Unreliable / needs human QA (HIGH burden)
- Guaranteeing compliance against a 100+ item RFP checklist (omission = disqualification).
- Anything legally consequential (UPL, submissions, certifications).
- Facts about a specific company not present in source material (hallucination).
- Pricing/commitments.
- Real-time judgement in live negotiations.

## Implication
- Bid Desk: AI can produce ~70-90% of DRAFT, but the last 10-30% (compliance + company-specific accuracy) is where the liability sits -> human QA is unavoidable and is the founder's binding time cost. **This may break the 1.6 hrs/₹1L assumption.**
- Signal Desk / Research Desk: output is informational (low liability) -> AI can run end-to-end; residual risk = quality/relevance -> churn.
- Rule: the more legally consequential the output, the LESS the AI-leverage claim can be trusted.
