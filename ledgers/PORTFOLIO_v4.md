# PORTFOLIO v4 (2026-10-02 — post investment-committee cycle)
Two candidates were put on trial. Both were killed as framed. New leading candidates came from unrelated markets.

## KILLED THIS CYCLE
**Bid/Proposal Desk — KILLED as framed.** Five independent failure axes [EVIDENCE]:
1. Value sits in client-specific facts an outsider can't supply ("they don't have your corporate knowledge"; vendor's own admission AI "cannot produce facts about your organisation… it is inventing one").
2. Trust-gated: buyers take referred, known vendors; cold vendors "looked like a scam".
3. Hidden firm/security gate repeated: CUI/ITAR/FCL and vendor onboarding (DPAs, security questionnaires) exclude an India solo from gov/defence.
4. Hallucination → disqualification: 42% of proposal pros name it #1 risk; a live DISA RFP test had tools invent evaluation criteria and miss DFARS 252.204-7012; 3 identical AI bids rejected by one council; compliance is binary.
5. Business-hours deadline coupling ("late is 100% a loss") incompatible with a full-time job.
   Category commoditised from both sides: platforms (Responsive from $10k; Loopio ~$20k/yr; AutogenAI $39.5M raise; GovDash) and offshore labour ($10–30/hr).
   **Survives only as:** back-office "shred + compliance matrix + first draft" subcontract to existing proposal shops ($15–30/hr) — a labour gig, not a productised desk. All prior quantitative claims (1.6–2.1 hrs/₹1L, $2–8k, 33 hrs/RFP, 168/152 jobs) = **UNVERIFIED**.

**Signal Desk — KILLED as framed.** [EVIDENCE]:
1. The exact product is already sold at ⅕ the price: Argus Intel $99–199/mo, Spyglass $29/mo, IndustryLens €59–249, Competiflow $19–99, Analook $5–29.
2. The buyer with budget is enterprise and procurement-gated (Klue median $30k/yr; Crayon $15–60k; procurement decides 53% of the time; only 8.4% of Crayon customers <50 staff).
3. SMB CI budget is ~zero (12% have zero CI budget; <half of small businesses near $25k).
4. Free DIY stack displaces it (Visualping + Google Alerts + LLM + Notion; "we built 80% of what they wanted $30k for").
5. Retention is catastrophic for AI-native subscriptions (GRR 40% vs 82% traditional; >$250/mo ~70%).
6. Valued output is "the so what" = human judgment, not monitoring.
   **Founder-hours claim overstated 5–10× [INFERENCE]:** ₹1L ≈ $1,163/mo; at $150/mo you need ~8 clients × 3–8 hrs = 23–62 hrs.
   **Survives only as:** white-label monitoring back-end for consultants ($100–300/mo), or a vertical productised service (breaks the low-hours thesis).
