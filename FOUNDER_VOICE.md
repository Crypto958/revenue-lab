# FOUNDER VOICE PROFILE (seed; learn from approved messages)
Defaults until corrected by approved/sent samples:
- Tone: direct, professional, low-hype, human. No flattery, no filler.
- Greeting: "Hi {name}," (not "Dear", not "I hope this finds you well").
- Length: 60-120 words for cold outreach; shorter for follow-ups.
- Structure: trigger/observation -> why it matters to them -> one specific ask -> sign-off.
- Credentials used sparingly and truthfully: FCCA, MCSI, ~12 yrs internal audit/model risk/financial services (ex-Wells Fargo). Never invent.
- CTA: one low-friction question (e.g., "Worth a 15-minute call?", "Are India-based remote candidates eligible?").
- Sign-off: "Abhishek" (+ LinkedIn link). Email: fca.abhi007@gmail.com
- Banned phrasings: "I hope this email finds you well", "came across your impressive profile", "explore synergies", "would be thrilled", "keen to leverage", "unique blend", "dynamic landscape", "esteemed organization".
