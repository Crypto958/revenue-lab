# PRODUCTISED OFFER (derived from observed repeating pain)
## The repeating finding across 32 Hyderabad businesses
Nearly every one fails the SAME step: **nothing acknowledges an enquiry instantly**. Booking pages are decorative (no form behind the CTA), enquiries land in a Gmail/WhatsApp inbox with no auto-reply, callbacks happen only in business hours, and nothing follows up. That is ONE problem, not 32.
## Product: "Enquiry Response System" (outcome: no enquiry left unanswered)
- Instant acknowledgement for website form / WhatsApp / missed call
- Simple qualification (what they need, when)
- Booking/appointment confirmation + reminder
- Follow-up sequence for quotes/enquiries that go quiet
- Post-service review request
- A one-screen pipeline: NEW -> CONTACTED -> QUALIFIED -> QUOTED -> BOOKED -> LOST
AI is the delivery mechanism. We sell: faster response, fewer missed leads, more bookings, more reviews.
