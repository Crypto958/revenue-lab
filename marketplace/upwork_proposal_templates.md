# UPWORK PROPOSALS (bidding on live jobs — not cold email)
Use when a client has posted a lead-list / data-enrichment / creative job. 60–110 words, specific to their post, one CTA.

## H2 — lead list job
> Hi — you need {N} {ICP} contacts. Two questions so I don't guess: (1) which titles count as the buyer, and (2) do you need emails verified, or is a best-effort list fine?
> I build and enrich lists to your ICP (company + contact + firmographics), de-dupe, and hand over a CRM-ready CSV/Sheet. Recent lists delivered in 3–4 days.
> Happy to send a free 20-lead sample first so you can judge quality before committing.

## N1 — ad creative job
> Hi — you're looking for ad creative that beats fatigue. Quick question: which is the primary goal right now, more variants for testing, or reworking the winning angle?
> I deliver organised variant sets (hooks + angles, Meta/TikTok sized) with a note on what each tests, so the results are readable.
> I can send 3 sample variants for one of your products first — no charge — so you can judge before hiring.

## Rule
Never bid on jobs outside H2/N1. One CTA only. No fabricated experience or client names.
