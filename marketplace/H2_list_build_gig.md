## GIG DESCRIPTION
> Building a cold-outreach list eats hours, and the tools that help (Clay, Apollo) bill $149–800/mo whether or not the list converts.
> I build the list for you: find companies matching your ICP, find the right person, enrich the record, de-dupe, and hand you a clean CSV/Sheet ready for your sending tool.
> Tell me your ICP (industry, size, geography, job titles) and any exclusions. If you're not sure of your ICP, add the ICP research add-on and I'll define it with you first.

## DELIVERY SOP (AI does ~85%)
1. Lock ICP (AI drafts from your brief; you approve).
2. Source candidates from **public/permissible sources** (company sites, public directories, professional networks). Do NOT use sources that breach a site's terms.
3. Identify the right contact per company (decision maker / functional owner).
4. Enrich: size, industry, location, tech, funding.
5. De-dupe + QA pass.
6. Verify emails with a **verification tool** (see blocker) — or label as best-effort.
7. Format CSV/Sheet; deliver with a short source note.

## HONEST BLOCKERS (must be resolved before promising 'verified')
- Email **verification** needs a tool (e.g. a verifier API) — small monthly cost → **requires Abhishek's approval to spend**.
- Without it, market the tier as "best-effort, unverified" — cheaper and still sellable, but expect some bounce complaints.
- Never claim an email is verified unless a verifier actually confirmed it.
